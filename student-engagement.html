Writers & Debaters Society

WDS in Action

"I speak, I write, therefore I am." The WDS runs the region's flagship debating competition and represents member schools in public speaking and literary discourse.

Luv FM / ARSRC High School Debate

The region's marquee inter-school debate competition, run by Luv FM in partnership with the Ghana Education Service, the Conference of Assisted Secondary Schools (CHASS), and the ARSRC's Writers & Debaters Society. Schools compete through a knockout bracket — Round of 16, quarterfinals, semifinals, and a grand finale — broadcast live on radio and streamed on Facebook and MultiTV's Joy Learning / Joy Prime channels.

  • Format: knockout rounds building to a live-broadcast grand finale
  • Organizers: Luv FM, GES, CHASS, and ARSRC WDS
  • Notable finalists over the years: Prempeh College, Kumasi Wesley Girls SHS, St. Monica SHS, T.I. Ahmadiyya SHS, Kumasi High School, PRESEC Bompata, and Yaa Asantewaa Girls SHS

Literary Group

LG in Action

The Literary Group Executive Committee (LIGEC) coordinates creative and performing-arts talent across member schools, anchored by two signature annual events.

Talefest

ARSRC's flagship talent showcase, built to educate and entertain in equal measure — celebrating student creativity while fostering unity across schools. Schools go through a preliminary round hosted at different member schools each year before qualifying for the regional finals.

  • Categories: poetry, drama, choreography, music, and cultural/traditional dance
  • Recent prelim & finals venues: Nsutaman Catholic SHS, Yaa Asantewaa Girls SHS, and Prempeh College
  • Participating schools include Kumasi Wesley Girls SHS, Osei Tutu SHS, Kofi Agyei SHTS, Barekese SHS, and Jachie Pramso SHS, among many others

Ashanti (High Schools) Music Fiesta

A regional showcase for school bands and choirs, launched by the Ashanti Regional Education Directorate in partnership with ARSRC under the theme "Music Without Borders: United in Harmony" — built to promote unity through music and give talented students a formal stage for their creativity.

  • Held at venues including Prempeh College and the Baba Yara Sports Stadium
  • Participating schools include Prempeh College, Yaa Asantewaa Girls SHS, St. Monica's SHS, Kumasi Wesley Girls SHS, Kumasi Girls SHS, Asanteman SHS, Kumasi Academy, Osei Tutu SHS, and Namong SHTS
