About ARSRC

A student council built around one regional voice.

The Ashanti Regional Students’ Representative Council is the umbrella body coordinating senior high school student leadership across the Ashanti Region.

Meet the executivesExplore engagement
Who we are

Leadership, representation, and opportunity.

Founded in 1997, ARSRC connects schools, student leaders, educational stakeholders, and young people around shared responsibilities and constructive action.

Aims & objectives

To serve as a mouthpiece for second-cycle students, support functioning SRCs, improve information flow, and develop responsible future leaders.

Our constitution

The constitution provides the framework for representation, accountability, elections, and the responsibilities of the Council.
